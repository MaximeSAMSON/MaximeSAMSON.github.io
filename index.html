About me

I'm Maxime Samson, a first-year engineering student at ENSEEIHT in Toulouse, in the Fluid Mechanics, Energy and Environment track. I came to engineering through physics: a CUPGE preparatory programme in physics and chemistry, then a bachelor's degree in fundamental physics at Université Paul Sabatier.

What drives me is aerospace propulsion, from turbojets to rocket engines, and the fluid mechanics behind it. I want to start my career in R&D and design engineering in the French and European aerospace industry, and later move into project management.

Outside class, I work summers as a lifeguard, tutor first-year students in mathematics, run, mix as a DJ and take salsa lessons.

Research and projects

  1. January 2026

    Research Intern

    ISAE-SUPAERO, DEOS Department, Toulouse

    Measured RF components with a vector network analyser (VNA): antennas, filters, attenuators, circulators, couplers, waveguides and cavities. Explored a PhD topic: time-reversal plasma ignition.

  2. Course project

    Simulation of the Artemis II free-return trajectory

    Numerical simulation in Python

    From a fixed-Moon two-body model to the full figure-eight trajectory around the Moon, integrated with a hand-written RK4 scheme and animated with Matplotlib in Jupyter.
